## Research Direction

My research focuses on grasp-force sensing for soft robotic hands and fingers. I study how structural deformation, visual observations, and data-driven methods can be used to infer external forces.

## Core Question

:::{important}
How can the position, direction, and magnitude of contact forces be inferred from observable deformation without directly embedding force sensors in a soft structure?
:::

## Research Topics

- Structural modeling of soft fingers
- Grasp force sensing and force inference
- Inverse problems under sparse observations
- Simulation-to-real domain-gap correction
- Graph neural networks and structured representations
- Visual tracking and uncertainty sensing under occlusion
